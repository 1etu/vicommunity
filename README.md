Notes
-----

1. This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto and GTA VI are their trademarks.
2. We do not condone piracy.
3. The forum is for showing what you got running with your own copy of the game: screenshots, logs and write-ups.
4. No download links. Not to the game, its files, keys, dumps or leaked builds, and no directions to any of them. Such a post is deleted and the account is banned.
5. The site hosts no game files.
6. The attempts listed are other people's public source code. Each one expects files from a copy you own. A place on the list is not a recommendation.
7. Only links to github.com and gist.github.com are published. A message with any other link is refused, and its author gets a notice with what they wrote.
8. A post is a title, text and up to three photos. Photos are converted on upload and their metadata, location included, is removed.
9. Login is through GitHub only. We ask GitHub for no permissions and keep your GitHub id and name. No email, no password.
10. No JavaScript, no ads, no analytics. Avatars load from GitHub.
11. One post a minute, one comment every thirty seconds.
12. Markup and slurs are refused.
13. Moderators delete what breaks the rules, close threads and ban accounts.
14. A bot check runs in front of the site. It works without JavaScript.
15. On the day an official PC version ships, the tracker is frozen. The site stays up as an archive.
16. The full posting rules are pinned at the top of the community page.
